# Business discovery

Read `sales-agent/README.md` and `sales-agent/compliance/compliance-policy.md` first.

## Targets (current)
Sectors: independent restaurants/cafés, barbers/hairdressers, local trades, independent retail, gyms/studios, cleaning firms, small professional services.
Areas: anywhere in the UK (George, 2026-11-17). Expanding to new towns or regions needs no further approval. West Yorkshire remains the home patch.

## Method
1. Use WebSearch with sector + town queries, plus "Ltd" / "limited" to bias towards companies (most small businesses in these sectors are sole traders and can't be cold-emailed).
2. Good sources: the business's own site, Companies House (find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk), trade bodies (FMB, Gas Safe, NAPIT, Checkatrade profile pages for the business's own listing), local council/BID directories.
3. Do not scrape Google Maps or directories against their terms. No CAPTCHA or login bypassing. Respect rate limits.
4. Exclude early: national chains/franchises, businesses already in the CRM (search by domain and name), anything on the suppression list.

## Output per candidate (to CRM as Status = Research)
Business name · sector · town · website URL (or "none found") · source URLs · research date · first impression of the opportunity · confidence (high/medium/low).

Aim for 10–20 candidates per session. Quality beats count — stop early if the remaining ones are weak.
