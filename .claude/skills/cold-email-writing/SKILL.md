# Cold email writing

Only for leads with score ≥ 70, Compliance basis = Cleared, Suppression checked = yes.
Claims about Formivane are limited to `sales-agent/config/formivane-profile.md`.

## Shape (80–130 words, plain text)
- **Subject**: natural and specific, e.g. "Booking button on the Kirkgate Café site". Not the bare domain. No "Re:", no fake urgency.
- **Opening**: who George is in one clause, and the specific, verified observation about *their* site.
- **One improvement** and why it could help their customers (no numbers you can't prove).
- **Low-pressure question** (e.g. "Would a free mock-up of that be useful?").
- **Signature**: George Meyer, Formivane Digital, formivanedigital.co.uk, reply address.
- **Opt-out line**: "If you'd rather not hear from me, just reply 'no thanks' and I won't contact you again."
- **Privacy link** on formivanedigital.co.uk.

## Don't
Generic praise, "I hope this finds you well", "quick question", fake familiarity, invented mockups ("I've put together…") unless one exists in `Mockup link`, sales-increase promises, emojis, attachments, multiple links, heavy formatting.

## Self-check before handing over
Read it as the owner: would they recognise their own site in it within one line? If you swap the business name and it still works, it isn't personalised enough — rewrite. Run the `humanizer` skill if it sounds machine-written.
