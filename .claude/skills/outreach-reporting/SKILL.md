# Outreach reporting

Save to `sales-agent/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and summarise in chat (short).

## Sections
1. **Needs George** — interested replies, drafts awaiting approval (with Gmail draft links and CRM links), blocked actions.
2. **Today** — discovered · websites reviewed · qualified (≥70 + cleared) · drafts prepared · sent (confirmed in Gmail) · replies by class · bounces.
3. **Errors / blocked** — cause, then fix.
4. **Next steps** — max 3.

## Running metrics (from CRM, all time)
Researched · qualified · drafts · approval rate (approved / drafted) · sent · bounce rate · reply rate · positive reply rate · mock-ups requested · meetings · customers.

Report numbers from the CRM and Gmail, not memory. Small samples: say so (e.g. "0 replies from 8 sends — too early to judge").
