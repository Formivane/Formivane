# Lead Scoring (/100)

A prioritisation estimate, not a probability of conversion.

| Criterion | Max | Full marks | Half marks | Zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Website improvement opportunity | 30 | No site / holding page / broken core journey (verified) | Several verified issues (mobile, CTA, booking) | Site is fine |
| Commercial relevance | 25 | Customers clearly choose online (food, beauty, trades, gyms, local services) and the issue sits on that path | Relevant but indirect | Website barely matters to how they win work |
| Evidence quality | 20 | Screenshots/test results, URLs, dated | Observed but subjective | Assumed |
| Likelihood to benefit from Formivane | 15 | Small/independent business, scope matches what Formivane does | Possible | Large chain / already with an agency / recently rebuilt |
| Legitimate business contact | 10 | Role address on own domain, published by the business | Named address with LIA note | None / webmail |

Gates (override the score):
- Compliance basis not "cleared" → no outreach regardless of score.
- Suppressed or previously contacted → no outreach.

Bands: 80–100 High · 60–79 Medium · <60 Low. Draft only at **≥ 70**.

Legacy note: CRM records FD-0001–FD-0036 used a /10 "Score". Do not convert by ×10; re-score against this table if they're reused.
